# D5 DRAFT — The Ending: "The succession is safe — for now" (three shades)

*A work of political fiction. All persons, offices, and organizations are fictitious.*

**Status:** DRAFT for the user's voice pass. Canon spine: the M2 closing sequence in
`Docs/MISSION_SCRIPTS.md` (Obj_Escape → on complete → closing narration → victory
card). Canon lines are reproduced **verbatim and unmarked**; new material is tagged
**[EXT]** so it can be stripped without touching canon. Nothing in
`MISSION_SCRIPTS.md` was modified.

**What this file adds:** three full recordable versions of the ending —
**BITTER**, **AMBIGUOUS** (the canon shade, widened), and **DEFIANT** — each
answering the question the queue item asks and canon leaves open: *what does the
win cost her?* All three occupy the identical slot, keep the same canon buttons,
and end on the same victory card, so any one drops in without touching M2 code.

---

## Binding constraints honored in all three versions

- **Tone (STORY_BIBLE):** "Le Carré restraint over Bond spectacle." No triumph, no
  score swell, no speech. The ending is "a victory that fixes nothing permanently."
- **GDD §Design pillar 4:** "No triumphant regime change; a fragile constitutional
  breathing space bought at cost. The whole game is tuned to earn that ambivalence."
  Therefore even DEFIANT is not *victorious* — it is unbowed, which is different.
- **Azadi is alive.** Canon: he takes a dart, not a bullet, and is "awake enough to
  smile." Every version must carry the knowledge that he wakes up.
- **Yas answers in half-sentences.** VO is the one place she is allowed whole
  thoughts — because no one is listening. That is the device; don't spend it twice.
- **Restraint rule (binding, MISSION_SCRIPTS Appendix):** no line names a real
  person, state, or organization. Held throughout.

## VERSION 1 — BITTER

*The win is indistinguishable from the crime. She is not sad; she is precise, which
is worse. Flattest delivery of the three — no catch in the voice anywhere.*

`M2_GATE_01` → `M2_GATE_02` (canon, verbatim)

**[EXT — VO over the drive]**

> **YAS (VO):** `M2_END_BIT_01` There's a captain who smokes by the cypress because his wife thinks he quit. In the morning, someone will ask him how a keycard walks out of a palace by itself. He'll pat his pocket. He won't have an answer, and the men asking will be very patient with him.

> **YAS (VO):** `M2_END_BIT_02` My father stood up in that room and asked twelve men to read their sins out loud and vote anyway. I took the sins home in a case instead. He'd call that the difference between us, and he'd be too kind about it. He's always too kind about it.

> **YAS (VO):** `M2_END_BIT_03` Every name. Every debt he thought he owned. It's in my lap now, and it weighs exactly what it weighed in his drawer — nobody warns you about that part. A thing like this doesn't care whose hands are on it. It only cares that they're steady.

> **YAS (VO):** `M2_END_BIT_04` He's alive because I decided he should be. Somebody will call that mercy. It wasn't mercy. I just didn't want to learn what I'd be on the far side of it — and now I get to wonder for the rest of my life whether that was restraint or a failure of nerve, and I will never once be able to tell.

`M2_CLOSE` (canon, verbatim)

> **YAS (VO):** *(no smile; the flattest possible reading — she is agreeing with a fact, not softening it)* For now.

`M2_CARD`

**What it costs her:** the moral distinction. She set out to stop the man with the
inventory and ends the night holding the inventory. Shirazi is the receipt: an
ordinary man who will be broken over a card he never lost.

## VERSION 2 — AMBIGUOUS *(the canon shade, widened)*

*This is canon's own ending, kept intact, with two [EXT] beats inserted to widen the
cost without resolving it. Recommended default if only one version ships: it is the
one the tone bible was written for.*

`M2_GATE_01` → `M2_GATE_02` (canon, verbatim)

> **YAS (VO):** My father dared a patient man to open his files. I stole the files instead. That's the difference between us — he'd rather be right in the morning; I'd rather be gone by then.

> **YAS (VO):** Azadi was correct about one thing. Nobody wins a succession. You steal a week. You hand it to twelve tired men in a cold room and hope they spend it better than the last week they were given.

**[EXT — insert here; strips cleanly]**

> **YAS (VO):** `M2_END_AMB_01` A week. I keep turning the word over. A week is nothing. A week is also every distance my father has ever crossed on foot, in the dark, alone, and arrived.

> **YAS (VO):** The dossier is out of his drawer. The Council will convene again, and this time they'll read their sins with the doors open — because I took away the man who was going to read them out one at a time.

**[EXT — insert here; strips cleanly]**

> **YAS (VO):** `M2_END_AMB_02` *(a beat, looking at the case, not the road)* Unless they don't. Unless twelve tired men look at an open drawer and decide the drawer was the only thing keeping them polite.

> **YAS (VO):** *(a breath)* It fixes nothing. It buys everything a little more time.

`M2_CLOSE` (canon, verbatim)

> **YAS (VO):** *(almost a smile)* For now. *(canon delivery — unchanged)*

`M2_CARD`

**What it costs her:** certainty. She cannot tell whether she has saved the republic
or removed the last thing frightening it into good behaviour, and the game does not
tell her — or the player — either.

## VERSION 3 — DEFIANT

*Not triumphant — unbowed. She refuses Azadi's arithmetic rather than disproving it.
Slightly faster cadence; the only version where she is looking forward through the
windscreen instead of back in the mirror. The cost is that she has chosen to keep
going, and she knows exactly who pays for that.*

`M2_GATE_01` → `M2_GATE_02` (canon, verbatim)

**[EXT — VO over the drive]**

> **YAS (VO):** `M2_END_DEF_01` *A week*, he said. Like it was a small thing, and like handing it to me was a kindness. He was going to use his week to bury a republic without raising his voice. I'll take mine.

> **YAS (VO):** `M2_END_DEF_02` Nobody wins a succession — fine. Then nobody loses one either, not tonight, not permanently, and he can lie in that palace with a dart in his shoulder and do the arithmetic himself. He's had thirty years of arriving early. Let him find out what it's like to be late.

> **YAS (VO):** `M2_END_DEF_03` He knows my face now. My name. By morning it'll be on a list in a building with no sign on it, and my father will be a man with a daughter on that list — which is the thing I did to him tonight, and I did it on purpose, and I'd do it again before the car reaches the road.

> **YAS (VO):** `M2_END_DEF_04` It fixes nothing. I never expected it to fix anything. I expected it to *start*.

`M2_CLOSE` (canon, verbatim)

> **YAS (VO):** *(not a concession — a promise, half under her breath)* For now.

`M2_CARD`

**What it costs her:** her father's safety and any road back. She has spent the one
protection she had — being Hossein Rahimi's daughter, the name that opened the gates
— and she spends it knowingly, on the first night of a much longer war.

## COMPARISON NOTES (for the taste call — NEEDS-USER)

- **BITTER** is the most le Carré and the most likely to be the *right* ending for a
  game whose thesis is that the drawer is the problem, not the man. Risk: after a
  boss fight the player has just won, four beats of self-indictment can read as the
  game scolding them. Mitigation — it never blames the *player's* choices, only
  Yas's position; every line is about what she now holds, not what she did wrong.
- **AMBIGUOUS** is canon and needs the least defending. It is also the only version
  whose final image is a question the sequel can answer either way. If the
  prototype ships to strangers with no Act II context, this is the safe pick.
- **DEFIANT** is the only version that gives the *player* an emotional payout for
  the fight they just finished, and the only one that seeds Mission 3 (GDD: Act I
  ends with "Azadi now hunting Yas by name" — `M2_END_DEF_03` plants exactly that).
  Risk: closest to Bond, and it makes Yas's willingness to endanger her father
  explicit, which is a bigger characterization commitment than canon has made.
- **They are not mutually exclusive.** The GDD's full-campaign finale already calls
  for "several *shades* of the same fragile win" scaling to a restraint tally. These
  three are usable as the prototype's single ending *and* as the shade-template for
  Mission 12's epilogue — same spine, three VO bodies, selected by tally.

## CANON FLAGS (approve or cut — automation does not decide these)

1. **Delivery of "For now."** The line is canon and locked in all three versions;
   canon's stage direction is *(almost a smile)*. BITTER and DEFIANT re-read that
   direction (flat / promise) without changing a word. If the direction is also
   canon-locked, AMBIGUOUS is the only compliant version — flagging rather than
   assuming.
2. **Shirazi's fate** (`M2_END_BIT_01`). Canon establishes the captain, the wife,
   and "he'll pat his pocket at the gate and blame the tailor" (M0). That an
   investigation falls on him is **new** — a strong cost beat and a possible
   Mission 3 thread, but it is an invention. Strips cleanly.
3. **Yas endangering her father knowingly** (`M2_END_DEF_03`). Canon has her aware
   of the stakes; it has never had her *accept* them out loud. This is the largest
   characterization claim in the file.
4. **"Twelve tired men might prefer the drawer"** (`M2_END_AMB_02`). Implies the
   Council is compromised enough to miss the blackmail. Consistent with the Council
   scene, but it darkens the Custodians beyond anything canon states.
