# VEIL OF SUCCESSION - Story Bible (planner canon, v1)
A work of political fiction. All persons, offices, and organizations are fictitious.
Setting: Tehran-inspired capital of an unnamed-in-dialogue Islamic republic, near future.

## Premise
The Supreme Guide, Ayatollah Mahdavi, is dying, and the constitution's succession
process runs through the Council of Custodians - twelve senior clerics and officials.
General Farrokh Azadi, commander of the Guardian Corps intelligence directorate,
is quietly engineering a "managed succession": a staged foreign-sabotage crisis that
will let him declare emergency powers, install a pliable cleric, and rule from behind
the veil.

## Protagonist
YASAMAN "YAS" RAHIMI, 29. Daughter of Custodian Hossein Rahimi, a moderate council
member. Educated abroad; recruited years ago by a back-channel network of officers
loyal to the constitution ("The Qanat"). Trained in infiltration and signals work.
Elegant, controlled, dry-humored - a 007 sensibility with a daughter's stakes:
her father's name gets her through gates her tradecraft alone never could, and if
she is caught, he hangs with her.

## Antagonist
GENERAL FARROKH AZADI, 58. War hero turned intelligence baron. Believes the republic
survives only through a strong hand - his. Keeps the succession dossier (blackmail
files on all twelve Custodians) in the Niavaran-style palace where the Council meets.

## Supporting
- HOSSEIN RAHIMI: Yas's father. Suspects the plot, cannot prove it, will not flee.
- "DARYA": Yas's handler in The Qanat, voice on the earpiece.
- CAPTAIN OMID SHIRAZI: palace guard captain whose keycard opens the inner doors.

## Prototype campaign (the 3 playable missions in this build)
M0 THE WALL OF ROSES - Night infiltration of the palace gardens; enter grounds,
   lift Shirazi's keycard, reach the palace doors.
M1 THE CUSTODIANS CONVENE - Inside: eavesdrop on the secret session where Azadi
   pressures the Council, then steal the succession dossier from the meeting chamber.
M2 THE PEACOCK THRONE - Azadi finds her in the throne room. Boss fight, then
   extraction through the gardens with the dossier.

## Tone
Le Carre restraint over Bond spectacle; the palace is beautiful and the rot is quiet.
Violence is optional where possible (tranq darts, not kills). The ending is a
victory that fixes nothing permanently: "The succession is safe - for now."
