*The throne room. The gold dais at the far end, the Peacock-style throne above

Lights rise one chandelier at a time, someone else's hand on the switches.
AZADI is already by the throne, unhurried, his back to her. He has been waiting

## VERSION A — HE SPEAKS FIRST (canon shape, expanded)

*His voice arrives before he moves. It is not raised. It doesn't need to be —
it is the only voice the room has ever needed.*

> **AZADI:** *(not turning at first)* The Rahimi girl. Educated abroad, they said. I didn't believe the rest of what they said. Now I do.
>
> **YAS:** *(even)* General.

**[EXT]**
> **AZADI:** *(a hand on the throne's arm, still not turning)* Do you know what this chair is? A copy. The real one was carted off by men with more patience than the men who owned it. Everything in this palace is a copy of something that was taken. *(a beat)* I find that instructive.
>
> **YAS:** Is this the part where you instruct me?
>
> **AZADI:** No. *(turning his head, only his head)* This is the part where I count what you're carrying.

> **AZADI:** You have something of mine.
>
> **YAS:** You have something of everyone's. That seems to be the arrangement.

**[EXT]**
> **AZADI:** *(almost pleased)* Your father's cadence. He sharpens a sentence the same way, then wonders why he bleeds. *(quiet)* I knew you when you were small, Yasaman. Your naming-day. He wept — a Custodian, weeping over a daughter, in front of soldiers. I remember thinking: that man has given the world a hostage.
>
> **YAS:** *(half-beat; the case is heavier than it was)* And you've spent thirty years collecting the ransom.
>
> **AZADI:** I've spent thirty years making sure it never comes due. There is a difference. You'll see it, at your age. Or you won't see anything at all.

> **AZADI:** *(finally turning, drawing his pistol without haste)* Your father dared me to open those files an hour ago. I'd hoped to spare him the sight of his daughter's name in them. — Put the case down, Yasaman. Walk out. I am, believe it or not, trying to be kind.
>
> **YAS:** You'll forgive me if I've had enough of your kindness for one evening.

**[EXT — staging]** *A long moment. He looks at her the way a man looks at
weather — something that will happen regardless, and must simply be dressed for.
He thumbs the safety. The sound is very small and it fills the room.*

> **DARYA (earpiece, low):** Yas — he won't let you walk. Cover, and make him work for it.

*Combat begins. AZADI Activate() — boss fight, MaxHealth 600, three phases.*

## VERSION B — SHE SPEAKS FIRST (alternate power dynamic)

*He lets the silence sit, back to her, and the silence is his weapon — every
second she stands mute in a sealed room, she is a girl caught, and both of them
know it. So she spends her one advantage: she refuses the silence.*

**[EXT]**
> **YAS:** *(even, before he has turned; walking, not stopping)* Working late, General. They told me you never leave the north wing.
>
> **AZADI:** *(not turning; if anything, pleased)* She speaks first. Your father would have waited — he lets the other man spend himself, it's his one gift. *(a beat)* You must get the nerve from your mother. I never met her. His loss was the one file I closed.
>
> **YAS:** *(flat)* Careful.
>
> **AZADI:** *(turning now, slow, hands still empty)* You see? Nerve. A dossier under one arm, twelve locked doors between you and the roses, and the child says *careful* — to me — in my own throne room.
>
> **YAS:** The Guide's throne room. I hear he's still alive.
>
> **AZADI:** *(a pause; the first line that costs him something)* ...Yes. That is the entire trouble with this week. Everyone I meet keeps insisting on the technicality.

> **AZADI:** You have something of mine.
>
> **YAS:** You have something of everyone's. That seems to be the arrangement.

**[EXT]**
> **AZADI:** *(gently, as if correcting her grammar)* The arrangement, Yasaman, is the only wall between this republic and the yard dogs. Your father knows it. That is why he argues with me in chambers and drinks with me in gardens — he has never once told me I'm wrong. Only that I'm *early.*
>
> **YAS:** Then I'm here to keep you punctual.

> **AZADI:** *(drawing his pistol without haste)* Your father dared me to open those files an hour ago. I'd hoped to spare him the sight of his daughter's name in them. — Put the case down, Yasaman. Walk out. I am, believe it or not, trying to be kind.
>
> **YAS:** You'll forgive me if I've had enough of your kindness for one evening.

**[EXT — staging]** *He sighs — an old man's sigh, entirely real, which is the
worst thing in the scene — and levels the pistol.*

> **DARYA (earpiece, low):** Yas — he won't let you walk. Cover, and make him work for it.

*Combat begins. AZADI Activate() — boss fight, MaxHealth 600, three phases.*

## COMPARISON NOTES (for the taste call — NEEDS-USER)

- **Version A** keeps the canon opener and plays Yas as controlled and
  receiving: Azadi owns the room, and her refusal at the end lands as the one
  thing he cannot budge. The naming-day beat gives the fight a private wound —
  he has known her whole life, and she learns it seconds before he shoots.
- **Version B** gives Yas (and the player, arriving with the dossier and some
  momentum) the initiative — and then shows why initiative doesn't matter
  against this man: he converts her nerve into evidence for his worldview
  without raising his voice. The "he's still alive" jab is the only line in
  either version that visibly costs him, which makes his calm afterward worse.
- Both versions preserve verbatim: his canon demand, the "arrangement"
  exchange, the "trying to be kind" offer + her refusal, and Darya's button.
- **Canon flags for the user:** (1) A introduces a naming-day history between
  Azadi and Yas's childhood — new backstory, strips cleanly if unwanted.
  (2) B mentions Yas's mother (dead, per "his loss") — the mother appears
  nowhere in canon; approve or cut. (3) A's "copy of a throne" beat implies the
  palace throne is a replica — set-dressing claim, harmless but new.
